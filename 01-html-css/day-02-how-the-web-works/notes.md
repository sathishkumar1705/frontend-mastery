## Notes

1. DNS lookup: Find the restaurant's address.
2. TCP handshake: Call the restaurant and establish communication.
3. TLS handshake: Make sure the conversation is secure.
4. HTTP request: Place your food order.
5. HTTP response: The restaurant confirms and delivers your order.
6. Browser renders the page: Arrange the food on your table, ready to enjoy.
